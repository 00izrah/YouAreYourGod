I stood at the end of this alley for about forty minutes. The light only reaches the ground for a few minutes either side of one o'clock, and on that day there was a cloud in the way for most of them.

## Exposing for the blade

The scene has maybe eight stops between the lit cobbles and the far wall. I exposed for the lit patch and let the walls fall where they fell. That is the whole decision: *protect the one highlight that matters, and trust the shadows to be dark.*

## Before and after

The file straight out of the camera is flat on purpose. All the contrast you see in the final frame was made afterwards, and the main job was not to crush the cobbles to black while pulling the walls down.

## What I left alone

The bicycle stayed in shadow. It would have been easy to lift it, and the picture would have lost the reason it was worth waiting.
