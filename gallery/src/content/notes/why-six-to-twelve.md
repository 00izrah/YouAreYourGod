---
title: "Why six to twelve"
date: 2026-06-30
summary: "On finishing a body of work: the case against the endless feed."
---

A feed has no ending, so nothing in it is ever finished. An exhibition has to be closed: you pick the frames, you put them in order, and then you stop.

Six is about the smallest number that can carry an argument. Twelve is about the most a person will look at attentively in one standing. Between those two numbers you have to be honest about what each frame is *doing* there.

Frames that did not make it into the room are not lost. They stay in the archive, filed by year, and sometimes they turn out to belong to a later room.
