I make photographs one room at a time. Each exhibition is a handful of frames, six to twelve, recorded over a few months around a single idea, then shown in order and kept.

The site is built to get out of the way: one frame at a time, a short plaque, and a dark wall. If a photograph needs more explanation than that, it is probably in the field notes.

*All photographs and wall text on this site are sample content, to be replaced with the real work.*
